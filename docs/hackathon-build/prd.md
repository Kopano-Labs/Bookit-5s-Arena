| R06 | Restore real inventory and authoritative scheduling | Court records have real IDs, owner mapping, verified prices/hours/closures. Imports are idempotent and produce dry-run/readback receipts. | R07 | Prove durable booking/customer persistence | An isolated sandbox browser booking produces one stored reservation with necessary customer details, court/time/total/status/reference; a new connection reads it; a fresh browser and operator dashboard show it. It remains after process restart and preview redeployment against the same sandbox store. Database target is explicit; no silent default-db ambiguity. |
| R08 | Make availability and booking consistent | Strict real-date/SAST/time-window validation. Concurrent overlapping requests, including different starts and durations, yield one valid owner of each court interval. Retries/double taps produce one reservation. Cancellation releases ownership; rescheduling swaps safely or preserves the old slot. Price is calculated server-side. No offline/local-only state is presented as booked. |
| R09 | Communicate the complete journey to customer and venue | Verify where customers discover/start booking and what they understand at each step. Show a reference only after a durable save and state booking/payment status correctly. The customer receives confirmation, next steps and authorized retrieval/cancel/reschedule access. The venue/client receives a booking alert tied to that same reference, with details needed to act and dashboard access; changes/cancellations reach both sides. Use verified configured channels, sandbox destinations and delivery receipts/retries/escalation. Queued/sent/delivered/read are distinct; a saved row or collected email is not communication success. |
| R10 | Respect the actual payment policy | Determine pay-at-venue versus online payment from current venue evidence. For venue payment, show total and amount due without invented checkout. If online payment is required, sandbox-only provider tests cover retries, callbacks and booking/payment consistency. Payment and cancellation/refund statuses cannot diverge silently. |
| R11 | Make the backend operational for staff | Existing operator dashboard uses the same reservation/customer records as public booking. Staff can find bookings, inspect status/contact/history, manage closure/manual blocks and supported lifecycle/payment actions with correct authorization. Changes propagate to public availability. Cross-customer access is denied. |
| R12 | Handle errors and customer information properly | Test unavailable inventory, DB outage, stale slot, timeout before/after commit, network loss, invalid contact, duplicate request and notification failure. Preserve recoverable selections; distinguish failed/unknown/saved. Minimize PII, redact logs, authorize guest management, bound retention/access and prevent exposed secrets. |
| R13 | Work on mobile, desktop and assistive input | Verify 360/390px mobile and 1440px desktop, plus narrower supported layouts; no clipped booking controls. Measure load/interaction cost and reduce decorative work that delays booking. Emulation is not physical-device or screen-reader certification. |
| R14 | Validate and deploy the complete journey | Repair relevant CI/lint blockers; use real sandbox browser/API/database/dashboard checks, concurrency and lifecycle evidence. | R15 | Deliver receipts and maintain consistency | Handoff includes current journey map, prioritized findings with reproduction/evidence/impact/confidence, confirmed versus suspected causes, repair sequence, test matrix, before/after captures, source/deployment refs, access gaps and ownership. | R16 | Complete the account welcome, contact profile and appointment-history experience | After a persisted native credentials/social account is created, send one branded welcome with useful on-domain Bookit book/manage/profile/history and venue contact/directions links. Guest booking remains quick/optional-account; guests have secure management access and any account linking requires verified identity. Distinguish account welcome from booking confirmation and venue booking receipt. |

## Proposed thresholds and unresolved policy

The 60-second usability target, >=44px controls and candidate test viewports are proposed acceptance targets, not recorded client measurements. Court rates/hours, contact minimum, hold duration, payment policy and cancellation cutoff require current venue/source evidence. Existing source has differing management-policy descriptions; do not pick a cutoff silently.

## Release Proof Points

The demonstration must show the whole communication chain: the customer reaches the booking entry, completes the attractive native flow, receives confirmation/management access; the venue receives the booking alert, finds the same durable record and observes changed availability. Changes/cancellations update both sides. Preserve redacted browser/network/database/dashboard/inbox/provider receipts. Production human booking and conversion improvement remain unmeasured until separately authorized/observed.
